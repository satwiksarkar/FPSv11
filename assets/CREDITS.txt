2. SOUND EFFECTS & AMBIENCE (assets/sounds/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All audio files are procedurally synthesized 16-bit 44.1 kHz PCM WAV files:

- gunshot.wav          : Bolt-action rifle muzzle blast (noise + resonant thump)
- plasma_shot.wav      : Rapid high-energy plasma repeater discharge (950Hz to 260Hz sweep)
- reload.wav           : Dual-click mechanical bolt slide
- door.wav             : Low-frequency grinding slide (rumble + friction)
- enemy_hit.wav        : Blunt impact audio thud
- death.wav            : Descending retro elimination tone
- pickup.wav           : Ascending two-tone acquisition chime (C5 -> G5)
- vent_explode.wav     : Heavy explosive detonation and resonant metal shatter
- level_clear.wav      : 4-note ascending triumphant fanfare (A4, C#5, E5, A5)
- factory_hum.wav      : Level 1 ambient drone (55Hz industrial transformer rumble)
- armory_hum.wav       : Level 2 ambient drone (65Hz cold airflow and metallic hum)
- core_hum.wav         : Level 3 ambient drone (40Hz sub-bass core reactor with cyclic alarm pulse)

Author: FPSLabGame Project
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
